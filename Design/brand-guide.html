01 · The mark

One continuous ribbon that reads “via”.

A single band falls into a v, rises through the i beneath a floating dot, and arches into an a before it settles to the right. Where it twists, the back of the ribbon shows. That twist is the moment of connection. The gradient travels from charcoal through ivory to champagne.

04 · App icon

Quiet on the home screen.

Full-bleed 1024 px artwork. iOS applies the mask. The ribbon spans about 76% of the width, and the mark’s bounding box (dot included) is optically centred. There is a single soft shadow and no other effects. The dark and tinted variants ship in the same asset catalog.

06 · Clear space & size

Give it room.

Clear space = dKeep at least one dot diameter (d) clear on every side of the mark or lockup. Use 2d where you can.
Minimum sizeGradient mark: 32 px / 24 pt wide (8 mm in print). Below that, use the monochrome mark down to 20 px wide. Horizontal lockup: 96 px wide. App icon: any system size.

07 · Usage

Do and don’t.

Do

Use the primary gradient on ivory or white.

Do

Use the on-dark version on black or charcoal.

Do

Use monochrome on photography and mid-tones.

Do

Use monochrome for small sizes, print and engraving.

Don’t

Stretch, squash or redraw the ribbon.

Don’t

Recolour it in blue or any off-palette hue.

Don’t

Rotate, tilt or animate it off its baseline.

Don’t

Place the gradient on mid-tones, or add outlines or glows.

09 · Components

Custom, calm and deliberate.

Viadere uses its own components (ViadereDesignSystem.swift) rather than stock controls. They share these principles:

  1. Custom over stockButtons, fields, toggles, selectors and approvals are drawn by Viadere. Use system UI only where the platform requires it, such as sheets, keyboards and share.
  2. Hairlines, not bordersEdges are 1 px at 10–12% opacity. Light mode gets one barely-there shadow; dark mode gets none.
  3. One primary actionThe charcoal (or ivory, in dark mode) button appears once per screen. Secondary actions sit on surface with a hairline.
  4. Champagne is earnedThe accent marks verification, focus and links. It never fills large areas.
  5. Deliberate approvalsSensitive actions use slide-to-approve or a Deny/Approve pair of equal size, with the facts listed above the decision.
  6. Generous space, tight typeUse a 24 pt screen margin, a 4-pt rhythm and seven type sizes. If it feels crowded, remove something.
  7. Restrained motionUse ease-outs of 0.16 s and critically damped springs, with subtle haptics. Respect Reduce Motion.
