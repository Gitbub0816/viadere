Verify with Viadere

Button artwork, mark and wordmark for businesses that let their customers verify with Viadere — on the web, in an iOS app and in an Android app. Use the live components where you can; use these files where a component cannot run (design mock-ups, documentation, emails that link to your own page).

The live buttons are in Server/Public/verify-button/v1/ (web), VerifyWithViadereButton in the business SDK (iOS) and Packages/android/viadere-verify (Android). Integration and security: docs/VERIFY-BUTTON.md.

Variants

Three variants, each in a light and a dark appearance. The live components follow the visitor's appearance automatically.

Filled · light

The default. Charcoal on light pages.

Filled · dark

The same button on dark pages: ivory.

Ivory

For dark, coloured or photographic backgrounds.

Outline · light

For busy light layouts and secondary placement.

Outline · dark

Ivory outline on dark pages.

Icon only

Only where space is tight and the context says "verify" (a toolbar next to a labelled field). It always carries the accessible name "Verify with Viadere".

Clear space and minimum sizes

½ h½ h
Button clear space = ½ heightKeep half the button's height clear on every side: no other button, link or text closer than that.
Minimum button36 px high with the label; never scale the artwork below that, and never shrink the label separately.
Mark and wordmarkClear space of one dot diameter on every side (built into the lockup files). Monochrome mark at least 20 px wide; gradient mark 32 px; horizontal lockup 96 px (Design/brand-guide.html).

Do and don't

DoShow the button where a customer decides to verify: an age gate, a checkout step, an account screen.
DoLink it only to a verification session your server created a moment ago (https://viadere.com/v/…), and decide on your server from Viadere's signed result.
DoPick the variant that contrasts with the background: filled on light, ivory on dark or imagery.
DoKeep the label exactly "Verify with Viadere".
Don'tRecolour the button or the mark, add gradients, shadows, outlines or a shield; don't use your brand colour as its background.
Don'tStretch, rotate, crop or re-type the artwork, or rebuild it from a screenshot.
Don'tPut it in emails, texts or ads, or link it to anything that is not a fresh session on your own verified domain: Viadere's app shows the site the request came from, and people are told to stop when it isn't the one they are on.
Don'tTreat a click, a return to your page or anything your front end says as a verification. Only the signed result counts.
