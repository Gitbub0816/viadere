# Security and operational risk register (OPS-001)

**Status:** v1, 2026-10-27. Seeded from the residual risks in `docs/SECURITY.md` §1 and the threats in `docs/SECURITY-TRUST-PLATFORM-SPEC.md` §15, plus infrastructure risks from this work package. Owners are **roles** until names are assigned (OPS-001 requires named owners before launch). Reviewed monthly by the security lead; every risk marked *Open* has a treatment owner and is re-rated after its control ships. New rows are added by PR.

**Scale.** Likelihood (L) 1 rare, 2 unlikely, 3 possible, 4 likely, 5 almost certain within 12 months of launch. Impact (I) 1 negligible, 2 minor (limited, recoverable), 3 moderate (a business or small user set harmed), 4 major (many users or regulatory notification), 5 severe (loss of trust anchor, mass harm or breach of identity evidence). Rating = L x I: High >= 15, Medium 8 to 14, Low <= 7. Treatment: mitigate, accept (named owner signs), transfer, avoid.
