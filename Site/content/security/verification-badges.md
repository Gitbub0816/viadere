Viadere shows a business as verified when it has passed a defined check. A badge is a statement about a check, not about the quality of the business.

## Verified business

::: disclosure
A verified business has completed Viadere's business-identity checks. Verification does not constitute an endorsement, guarantee of legitimacy, or confirmation of regulatory licensing.
:::

## Verified is not endorsed

Viadere distinguishes **verified business identity** from a business being **trusted or endorsed**. Today there is no endorsed tier. If one is introduced it will be a separate, separately explained mark, and a verified business will not be shown as endorsed.

## Verified age is not verified identity

::: disclosure
Where supported, Viadere can confirm that a person meets a specified age threshold without disclosing their date of birth. Availability and legal sufficiency vary by jurisdiction and service.
:::

::: disclosure
Viadere may verify selected identity attributes using supported credentials or verification providers. A verification result describes the attribute and assurance evaluated; it is not a universal guarantee of identity or eligibility.
:::

Verify is [planned](/products/verify/) and not available. Face ID on a phone unlocks a key on that device. It does not prove legal identity, and not every Apple Wallet user can present an ID.

## Evidence for every label

Every verification label and security claim on this site and in the product must map to documented evidence and an approved public disclosure. The build of this site fails if a banned or unsupported claim appears without that evidence. See [attestations](/security/attestations/).
