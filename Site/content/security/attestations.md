## Independently achieved

None yet. Viadere has not completed an independent audit, a certification or a third-party penetration test, and publishes no attestation. This page will list a report only once it exists, with its scope, date and auditor. [SECURITY: update when evidence exists.]

## Claims we do not make

Viadere does not describe any product as "unhackable". It does not say that anything is "anonymous": businesses see a per-business pseudonym, and Viadere itself holds account data. It does not say "zero knowledge", "government certified" or "bank compliant". It does not say a product is "HIPAA compliant". Whether an integration involves protected health information is decided with the business and its counsel before any handling.

We do not use the phrase "end-to-end encrypted" for a feature unless it is technically true for that named feature and independently supportable. Where it is not true we say so plainly: ordinary SMS is not end-to-end encrypted.

## How this is enforced

The code that builds this website runs an integrity check on every page. The build fails when a banned claim, an attestation term (a named standard or a certification claim) or a missing product status appears without a front-matter entry that points to documented evidence and records who approved it. Pages that mention SMS fallback must state that SMS is not end-to-end encrypted. Pricing pages must disclose the fee categories.

## Demos and future features

Illustrations on this site say they are illustrations. Planned products are labelled planned and described as intent, not as available features.
