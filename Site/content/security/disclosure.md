If you believe you found a vulnerability in Viadere, please tell us and give us a reasonable time to fix it before you publish.

::: callout
The security mailbox and any encryption key are not yet published. Until they are, the contact below is a placeholder. The file at [/.well-known/security.txt](/.well-known/security.txt) is the machine-readable source and carries the same placeholders.
:::

## How to report

- **Contact:** [SECURITY-CONTACT-PLACEHOLDER]
- **Encryption key:** [PGP OR OTHER KEY PLACEHOLDER]
- **Include:** what you found, steps to reproduce, the affected endpoint or screen, and any proof of concept. Do not include real customer data.

## What to expect

[COUNSEL AND SECURITY: acknowledgment time, triage time and fix targets by severity.] We will not ask you to keep a vulnerability secret indefinitely.

## Scope and rules

The binding terms are in the draft [Vulnerability Disclosure Policy](/legal/vulnerability-disclosure-policy/). In short: test only your own accounts and sandbox data, do not access or change other people's data, do not disrupt the service, and stop and report if you reach customer data. [COUNSEL: safe-harbor language.]
