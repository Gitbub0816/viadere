Viadere is free for consumers. You connect to a business on purpose, you choose what it may send, and you can disconnect at any time.

## Recognize a verified business

A request in the Viadere app or its notification names the business that is asking. Businesses that completed Viadere's checks are shown as verified.

::: disclosure
A verified business has completed Viadere's business-identity checks. Verification does not constitute an endorsement, guarantee of legitimacy, or confirmation of regulatory licensing.
:::

Verified does not mean endorsed or trusted by Viadere. It is not a guarantee that the business is honest or licensed. See [what the badge means](/security/verification-badges/).

## What a request tells you

Every request names the business, its purpose, any amount or location, when it expires and what happens if you approve. If a request is missing these, do not approve it, and report it.

## Notification controls

- Consent is **per purpose**: security, account service, transactional, support, marketing and rewards are separate. Turning off marketing does not turn off security notices.
- Viadere respects your system notification settings and Focus.
- You can disconnect from a business and see your connections.

## Privacy

Businesses see a per-business pseudonym, not your Viadere identity or phone number. Read the [Privacy Center](/privacy/).

## Verification choices

Where Verify becomes available, you choose what to prove. Verify is planned and not available yet.

## Accessibility

We aim for WCAG 2.2 AA on this site and in the app. See the draft [Accessibility Statement](/legal/accessibility-statement/).

## Lost or replaced device

Some credentials are protected on your device. Losing access to a device or recovery method may require re-enrollment or re-verification, and an approval key for a business is never silently replaced. Details will appear here as recovery is finalized. [PRODUCT: recovery flow description.]

## Text messages

If you use Viadere by text, ordinary SMS is not end-to-end encrypted. A text can show a secure link, but an approval of a sign-in or payment happens only in the app. See [safety and reporting](/consumers/safety/).
