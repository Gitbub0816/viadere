## Four warning signs.

- {icon:x} You were not trying to sign in or pay.
- {icon:x} The business name is not one you connected to.
- {icon:x} It asks you to approve something by text reply.
- {icon:x} It pressures you to act immediately.

A sensitive Viadere request is never approved by an SMS or RCS reply. If a text says otherwise, it did not come from Viadere's approval flow.
