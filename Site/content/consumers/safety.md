## Signs of a suspicious request

- You were not trying to sign in or pay.
- The business name is not one you connected to.
- It asks you to approve something by text reply.
- It pressures you to act immediately.

A sensitive Viadere request is never approved by an SMS or RCS reply. If a text says otherwise, it did not come from Viadere's approval flow.

## If you did not start it

Choose **No** or **This was not me** in the notification. A "not me" report counts toward that business's complaint rate, and a business whose rates are too high has its limits reduced automatically.
