## What money does not buy

Paying never changes the priority of your messages and never bypasses a customer's consent. A plan raises quotas only. Marketing and rewards never exceed normal priority, and urgent priority is reserved for security sign-in and authorization requests.

## Free tier

The default free tier has a daily limit on live interactions (currently 1,000). Sandbox traffic with a test key is never counted or billed. There is no payment processor integration yet.
