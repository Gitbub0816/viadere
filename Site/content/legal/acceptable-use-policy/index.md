## 1. Purpose

This policy lists conduct that is not allowed on Viadere. [COUNSEL: relationship to the Business Services Agreement and enforcement.]

## 2. Prohibited conduct

- Impersonating another business or person, or using a verified identity that is not yours.
- Showing text on an approval screen that is misleading, that starts with or mentions "Viadere" in a name, or that contains hidden control characters.
- Presenting a promotional message as an authentication or transactional message to avoid consent rules.
- Sending messages to people who have not consented, or ignoring STOP and opt-out requests.
- Using the platform to approve anything by text that must be approved in the app.
- Probing other businesses' data, or trying to correlate customers across businesses.
- Attempting to bypass rate limits, quotas, priority caps or consent checks.
- Collecting credentials, payment details or identity documents under false pretenses.
