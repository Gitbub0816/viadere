This Acceptable Use Policy (the "**AUP**") applies to every business, organization or person that uses the Viadere Business API, Dashboard or other business services ("**you**", "**Customer**"), and to everyone who uses them on your behalf. It is part of the [Business Services Agreement](/legal/business-services-agreement/) with [COUNSEL: LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("**Viadere**") and works with the [Messaging Consent and Content Policy Addendum](/legal/messaging-consent-content-policy-addendum/). In this policy, "**Business Workflow**" or "**Loop**" means an optional interactive workflow that a business builds from components and registered actions Viadere provides (a working name, [COUNSEL: pending trademark clearance]), "**Brand Kit**" means a business's optional appearance settings and uploaded marks, and "**Health Information**" and "**Healthcare Provider**" have the meanings in the Business Services Agreement (summarized in section 3.4). Viadere is a trust network: people connect to verified businesses, and the rules below protect them and protect the businesses that follow them.

## 1. Principles

1. **People choose who reaches them.** You may contact a person through Viadere only after they have connected to you and allowed the purpose.
2. **Be who you say you are.** Everything a person sees must truthfully identify you and what you are asking.
3. **Do not weaken protections.** Security controls, limits and consent checks exist for the people on the other end.
4. **Money does not buy priority or consent.** No plan or payment changes the priority of a message or bypasses a person's choices.
5. **The business is responsible.** You are responsible for what you send, the consent you hold and the laws that apply to your communications, and for what you build and what you do after a person acts on it.
6. **Viadere is not a place for health information or payments.** Healthcare providers may not use Viadere, no health information may pass through it, and Viadere never accepts or processes payment.

## 2. Prohibited conduct

You will not, and will not allow others to:

### 2.1 Identity and impersonation

- Impersonate another business, a person, Viadere, a government agency, a bank or any other entity, or use a verified identity, handle, code or domain that is not yours.
- Show text on an approval screen, a notification or a message that is misleading, that starts with or contains "Viadere" in a name, or that looks like a Viadere system message.
- Use hidden, invisible, bidirectional or other control characters, look-alike characters or any technique to disguise text.
- Register a handle, code or business name that is deceptive, infringes a mark, impersonates another business, or is confusingly similar to another business's.
- Misrepresent your verification status or use a verified badge to imply endorsement, licensing or that Viadere guarantees you.

### 2.2 Consent and unwanted messages

- Send messages to people who have not connected to you and allowed the purpose.
- Present a promotional message as an authentication, security or transactional message to avoid consent rules.
- Ignore STOP, opt-out or revoked consent, or contact a person on another channel to avoid an opt-out.
- Buy, rent, scrape or share lists of phone numbers or contacts for use through Viadere.
- Send messages that are not what the person expects, or at a frequency they would not reasonably expect.

### 2.3 Security approvals and authentication

- Ask for or accept an approval by text that must be approved in the app.
- Send approval requests that are unclear, that hide the amount, merchant, action or consequence, or that have been created to trick or pressure a person (including by repeated "prompt bombing").
- Use the approval service for something you did not originate, or relay another party's requests to a person.
- Fail to validate the signed approval result yourself before acting.
- Use Viadere to collect passwords, one-time codes for other services, recovery codes, payment details or identity documents under false pretenses.

### 2.4 Platform integrity

- Probe, scan or test other businesses' data, accounts or tenants, or attempt to access data you are not authorized to see.
- Attempt to correlate people across businesses, re-identify a pairwise reference, or build a cross-business profile of a person.
- Attempt to bypass, disable or manipulate rate limits, quotas, priority caps, cool-downs, freezes, consent checks or other protections, including by creating multiple accounts or workspaces to evade a limit or a suspension.
- Share, sell or expose API keys, webhook secrets, certificates or Dashboard sessions, or put keys in client-side code or public repositories.
- Use the sandbox with production personal data, or production keys in test.
- Interfere with, overload or disrupt the services or other customers' use of them.
- Reverse engineer, scrape or enumerate the directory, handles or codes.
- Use the Business API to build a competing service or for purposes it was not designed for.

### 2.5 Unlawful and harmful use

- Use Viadere in violation of law, including laws on communications, privacy, consumer protection, advertising, sanctions and export.
- Send, host or link to malware, phishing, spam or content that is fraudulent, deceptive, defamatory, harassing, threatening, hateful, sexually explicit or that exploits or endangers children.
- Facilitate fraud, money laundering, extortion, stalking, human trafficking, or violence.
- Infringe intellectual property or privacy rights.
- Use Viadere for emergency or safety-critical communications, or for activities where a failure could result in death or serious injury.

### 2.6 Data misuse

- Sell, share for cross-context advertising, or use data received through Viadere to discriminate against a person for exercising a right.
- Use the information you receive through Viadere for a purpose that is incompatible with the purpose the person allowed.
- Keep a verification result or sensitive attribute longer than your agreement allows, or use it for another purpose.

### 2.7 Business Workflows ("Loops")

- Build a Loop from anything other than the components and registered actions Viadere provides, or try to insert code, scripts, markup, links or other content the components do not allow.
- Publish, or try to put in front of a person, a Loop or a revision that Viadere has not approved, or change a Loop after approval in a way that avoids review.
- Hide, alter or imitate the "Built by" attribution, use text or styling that looks like a Viadere security screen, an approval, a system message, a government or a bank, or present a Loop as made or endorsed by Viadere.
- Ask a person, through a Loop, for Health Information, passwords, one-time codes, recovery codes, payment card or bank details, government identifiers or other sensitive information, or design a field to invite it.
- Make a Loop mislead a person about what it does, what it costs, who is responsible for it, or what will happen after they act, or promise an outcome (such as delivery or a result) that the Loop cannot guarantee.
- Pressure a person to turn on Loops or to allow your Loops, make a core service depend on using a Loop, or continue to show Loops to a person who has turned them off.
- Use a Loop to evade a consent rule, an opt-out, a limit or a suspension.

You are accountable for your Loops and for everything you do after a person acts in one. Viadere reviews every Loop and every revision before publication, and may refuse, pause, suspend or remove any Loop, or your ability to use Loops, at its discretion (Agreement section 3.7). A Loop that Viadere approved can still break this policy.

### 2.8 Brand Kits

- Upload an image that is not a PNG or JPEG file you have the right to use, or try to get another file type, a script or hidden data through the upload.
- Upload a mark, logo, name or artwork that you do not own or have a licence to use, or that infringes, imitates or is confusingly similar to another business's or Viadere's.
- Use a Brand Kit to imitate Viadere's own design or security language, to obscure a business name, verified badge or security control, or to make a request look like something it is not.

Viadere re-encodes uploads and may override or disable any part of a Brand Kit for accessibility, safety or security (Agreement section 3.8). You grant Viadere the licence described there, and Viadere may remove materials on a complaint.

### 2.9 Commerce and payments

- Ask for, collect or accept payment, card or bank details through any part of the Services, or describe Viadere as a payment provider, a checkout or the seller.
- Use a commerce connector other than your own Shopify store, or connect a store you do not control or are not authorized to connect.
- Use the Shopify feature for digital content or functionality used in the Viadere app, or for goods or services that are prohibited under section 3.1.
- Say or suggest that an Approval of an estimate, quote or terms is a payment, or use an Approval to take or imply payment.
- Describe a sale, price, refund, tax, shipping or dispute as Viadere's responsibility.

Viadere is never the merchant of record. The sale and everything about payment is between you and your customer, and Shopify's terms apply to Shopify's services (Agreement section 3.9).

### 3.4 Healthcare and Health Information are prohibited

Healthcare is banned on Viadere. This applies to every business, in every plan, and no written agreement, tier or addendum can approve an exception.

1. **Healthcare Providers may not use Viadere.** A "**Healthcare Provider**" is a person or organization that provides, arranges, bills for or is paid for health care, including a physician or dental practice, clinic, hospital, pharmacy, laboratory, telehealth service, health plan or health-care clearinghouse, a business associate of any of them, and any other business whose use of Viadere would involve Health Information. [COUNSEL: confirm scope, including wellness and fitness.]
2. **No Health Information through any feature.** "**Health Information**" is any information that relates to a person's physical or mental health or condition, the provision of health care to them, or payment for it, including protected health information under HIPAA and health data under other laws. You will not ask for, collect, store, send or act on it through any Viadere feature, including messages, approvals, rewards, Loops, forms and fields, attachments, templates, display text, metadata and webhooks, and you will not design anything that invites a person to provide it. Where a field or message could invite it, tell people not to share health details.
3. **No business-associate role.** Viadere does not accept Health Information. It is not a business associate, will not sign a business-associate agreement and does not offer a HIPAA-compliant service.
4. **Screening.** Viadere may screen applications, content and Loops, automatically and by review, for Healthcare Providers and Health Information. It may refuse an application, decline to approve content or a Loop, reclassify a business, and remove content. Viadere is not obliged to find a breach.
5. **Offboarding.** If Viadere reasonably believes a business is a Healthcare Provider, or that its use involves Health Information, Viadere may suspend it at once, remove its content and Loops, and terminate its agreement (Agreement sections 5.10 and 18.3). Viadere will where practical give a short period to export other data, and may delete Health Information without returning it.
6. **If you find it.** If you receive or send Health Information through Viadere by mistake, stop, tell [TRUST-CONTACT-PLACEHOLDER] at once, and do not use or share it. People can also report a business or a Loop that asks for it (section 6).

## 5. Monitoring and enforcement

### 5.1 How Viadere monitors

Viadere monitors the services for abuse using signals such as complaint, opt-out, denial and blocked-message rates, suspicious-request reports, unusual volumes or origins, failed verification and reports from people, carriers and providers. Viadere does not read message content to enforce this AUP except as needed to investigate a report, a security matter, a legal obligation or a carrier or provider request, and tries to limit what it looks at.

### 5.2 Responses

Viadere decides how to respond according to the seriousness, risk, history and intent. It may, without limiting other rights:

1. Warn you and ask for a fix.
2. Throttle your rate, reduce your quotas or lower your priority ceiling.
3. Require a corrective plan, extra verification, or an additional review.
4. Remove or reject content, a Loop, a Brand Kit or part of one, a handle, a code or a template.
5. Pause or remove a Loop, switch off Loops or Brand Kits for you, pause a Capability, a campaign, a channel or a key, or revoke keys.
6. Suspend your access to part or all of the services.
7. Terminate your account.
8. Notify affected people, carriers, providers or authorities, and cooperate with investigations.
9. Take legal action.

Viadere may act at once, without notice, where there is a risk to people or the service. Otherwise it will, where practical, tell you why and give you a chance to respond. Suspensions and terminations are also governed by section 8 and section 18 of the Business Services Agreement.

### 5.3 Handles and codes

Viadere may withdraw a handle for violation of this policy. A business code is never reassigned to another business.

### 5.4 Appeal

You may appeal an enforcement action by writing to [TRUST-CONTACT-PLACEHOLDER] within [14] days, with the reasons and supporting evidence. A reviewer who was not involved in the decision will respond within [10] business days. An appeal does not suspend the action unless Viadere agrees.

### 5.5 Repeat violations

Repeat or serious violations may lead to permanent termination. Viadere may refuse a new application from a person whose account was terminated for a serious violation.

## 8. Examples

These examples illustrate the rules. They are not a complete list.

| Situation | Allowed? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A bank asks a customer to approve a sign-in, showing the device, approximate location and time | Yes | A clear security request, with the customer's connection and consent |
| A retailer sends an order-shipped update to a customer who connected for transactional messages | Yes | Within the purpose the customer allowed |
| A retailer adds a 20 percent coupon to a sign-in approval request | No | Marketing combined with a security request, which hides a promotion in an authentication message |
| A business texts a coupon to a customer who allowed only transactional messages | No | Marketing needs separate consent for the channel |
| A business sets its display name to "Viadere Support" | No | Impersonates Viadere |
| A business sends 20 approval requests in an hour to one customer | No | Prompt bombing; Viadere refuses more than the hourly limit |
| A support agent replies to a customer's question through the Viadere thread | Yes | A reply in a conversation the customer started |
| A developer tests with a `vdk_test_` key and sandbox customers | Yes | The sandbox is for testing |
| A developer posts a `vdk_live_` key in a public repository | No | Exposes a credential; rotate it at once and tell Viadere |
| A florist builds a Loop that asks which pickup window a customer prefers | Yes | A low-risk workflow built from Viadere components; it is reviewed before anyone sees it, and shows "Built by" the florist |
| A business that is a Healthcare Provider applies to send reminders | No | Healthcare Providers may not use Viadere (section 3.4) |
| A salon's Loop has a field that asks about a person's health | No | Health Information is prohibited (section 3.4) |
| A repair shop connects its Shopify store so customers can see order status and go to its checkout | Yes | Viadere shows status and hands off; the shop is the seller |
| A business offers payment through Viadere or collects card numbers in a Loop | No | Viadere never accepts or processes payments and is not a checkout |
| A contractor sends an "Approve estimate" request | Yes | It records consent to the estimate. It must not charge anyone or be described as a payment |
| A business uploads an SVG logo, or a logo it has no rights to | No | Only PNG or JPEG you have the right to use |
| A business changes a published Loop and shows the new version without review | No | Every revision needs Viadere approval before publication |
| A researcher tests their own account under the Vulnerability Disclosure Policy | Yes | Good-faith research within the rules |

## 9. Good practice

You are encouraged to: tell people at connection what you will send and how often; keep your handle, contacts and domain verification current; keep suppression and consent records; monitor your own complaint and opt-out rates in the Dashboard; use the least scope for each key; rotate keys on a schedule; and review your integration against the Documentation before you go live.
