## 1. Scope

This addendum applies when a business sends messages through Viadere on any channel: the Viadere app, RCS and SMS. [COUNSEL: relationship to the Business Services Agreement.]

## 2. Consent and purpose

Consent is recorded per purpose: security, account service, transactional, support, marketing and rewards. A marketing message requires marketing consent that covers the channel. Promotional messages must not be presented as authentication or transactional messages. ## 4. Opt-out, STOP and HELP

STOP and HELP are handled consistently on every text channel and follow carrier rules. A suppression list is kept. [COUNSEL: quiet hours where applicable and the exact keywords.]

## 5. Content rules

Text a business sends is shown with the business attribution. Business text may not impersonate Viadere or another business, and may not contain hidden control characters. See the [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use-policy/).

## 6. Channel limits and SMS fallback

Text channels carry only unauthenticated replies. A security-sensitive request is never approved by SMS or RCS and is replaced by a secure link to the app. When a request falls back to SMS, ordinary SMS is not end-to-end encrypted, and carriers and providers can read it.
