## 3. Purposes and legal bases

We use data to deliver and secure requests and messages, to keep records of decisions and consent, to prevent fraud and abuse, to bill businesses, and to meet legal duties. [COUNSEL: purposes mapped to legal bases where required.]

## 4. What businesses receive

A per-business pseudonym for you, your decisions and the messages you send to them. Not your Viadere identity or phone number.

## 5. What stays on your device

::: disclosure
Some credentials are protected on your device. Losing access to a device or recovery method may require re-enrollment or re-verification; some nonrecoverable credentials may need to be recreated with the original service.
:::

## 6. Age and identity checks

::: disclosure
Where supported, Viadere can confirm that a person meets a specified age threshold without disclosing their date of birth. Availability and legal sufficiency vary by jurisdiction and service.
:::

Verify is planned and not available. [COUNSEL: privacy impact assessment status for government-ID processing, biometric verification and age assurance.]

## 7. Security

::: disclosure

:::
