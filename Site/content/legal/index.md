## How legal pages are managed

- Every document has a **version**, an **effective date** (empty until counsel approves it), an **owner** and a **counsel review** status.
- A document cannot take effect before counsel review is approved.
- Each version keeps a permanent address, `/legal/{document}/versions/{version}/`. When a new version replaces one that took effect, the old version stays at its address and is marked superseded.
- A version that took effect is never edited or removed. A change is a new version.
