## 3. Credentials and security

You keep API keys and webhook secrets confidential, use the least scope needed, rotate them on a schedule and tell Viadere promptly of a suspected compromise. Viadere may revoke a key or suspend access. [COUNSEL: notice periods and responsibilities.]

## 4. Your responsibilities

::: disclosure
Viadere delivers and records authentication approvals. The business remains responsible for account access decisions and for independently validating applicable approval evidence.
:::

You are responsible for your messages, your consent records and complying with the laws that apply to your communications. Viadere is not responsible for deciding whether you may lawfully contact a person. [COUNSEL: allocation of sender and initiator responsibility by message type.]
