## Our goal

We aim for this website and the Viadere app to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. [COUNSEL: whether to state a conformance claim.]

## What we have done on this site

- A skip link, a visible keyboard focus style and a consistent heading structure.
- Navigation that works without scripts.
- Light and dark colour schemes with text contrast designed for AA, and support for the reduced-motion setting.
- A layout that works from a 360 pixel width without horizontal page scrolling. Wide tables scroll inside a labelled region.
- No tracking and no content that changes without your input.

## What we have not yet verified

This site has not had an independent accessibility audit and has not been tested with a full range of assistive technology. [DESIGN: record manual screen reader and keyboard testing here with dates.] The app's accessibility is assessed separately.
