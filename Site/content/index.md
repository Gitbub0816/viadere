## Who is this really from?

A text from an unknown number, a link you are not sure about, a request you cannot check. Viadere gives a business a **verified identity** and gives you a clear, structured request: who is asking, why, how urgent it is, and what happens if you say yes.

Businesses issue a request through one API. You decide on your phone, usually from the notification itself. The business receives a signed result it can check for itself.
:::
:::

## How it works

::: steps
::: card
### A business is verified
A business applies, and Viadere reviews it before it can reach anyone. Customers see a verified name, not just a phone number.
:::
::: card
### A clear request arrives
Confirm a pickup, approve a sign-in, authorize a payment. The notification shows the business, the purpose and the context.
:::
::: card
### You decide, the business checks
Approvals are signed on your device. The business validates the signature itself and stays responsible for its own access decisions.
:::

## Built on restraint

::: grid grid-3
::: card
### Identity you can read
Verified business identity is a defined check with a public meaning. It is not an endorsement. [How badges work](/security/verification-badges/).
:::
::: card
### Private by design
Sensitive keys stay on your device. Businesses see a per-business pseudonym, not your Viadere identity or phone number. [Privacy Center](/privacy/).
:::
::: card
### Honest about limits
Ordinary SMS is not end-to-end encrypted, and we say so wherever SMS fallback applies. [What we claim and what we do not](/security/attestations/).
:::
