::: section
::: prose
A Brand Kit lets a verified business bring its logo, a few colours and some artwork to its own space in the Viadere app: its page, its conversation and its rewards. It is appearance only.

::: callout
**Built, not released.** Brand Kit Studio, the image pipeline, review and signed delivery are built and tested on the server. Brand Kits are not available to any business or customer, the themed screens have not been run on a device, and no date is promised.
:::

## A short list, *on purpose.*
:::

::: grid grid-3
::: card
### {icon:sparkle} A few tokens
A primary colour, optional secondary, accent and surface colours, corner radius, border weight and a type scale. There is no setting for text, links, labels, layout or fonts.
:::
::: card
### {icon:doc} Reviewed images
A logo, a mark, a background and artwork, as PNG or JPEG only. Vector and markup files are refused. Each image is decoded, stripped of metadata and stored as a fresh copy.
:::
::: card
### {icon:clock} Schedules
Several named kits, with date windows in the business's time zone, a default and an optional rotation. The kit chosen for a request stays with it, so the look never changes mid-flow.
:::

## Style is never allowed to *change meaning.*
:::

::: grid grid-2 gap-lg
::: card
### {icon:eye} Legibility wins
A colour is kept only if text on it meets a contrast minimum. A colour that fails is replaced by the neutral one, and the business is told which.
:::
::: card
### {icon:lock} Protected elements
A kit cannot change what a button means, a risk disclosure, a permission prompt, the verified-business indicator, a destination or any security text.
:::
::: card
### {icon:user} A person approves it
A kit and each of its images wait for a Viadere reviewer. An edit makes a new version that waits again, while the last approved version keeps serving.
:::
::: card
### {icon:bolt} Neutral when in doubt
If a kit or an image cannot be verified or loaded, the app uses Viadere's neutral style. No approval, sign-in or alert waits for a kit.
:::
:::

- Decorative frames come only from Viadere's own list. A business cannot upload one.
- Viadere can take down one image or one kit, or switch Brand Kits off for one business or for everyone.
- A Brand Kit does not restyle the signed [Wallet pass](/products/wallet/). It styles the pass card inside the app only.

Core features do not depend on a Brand Kit: without one, a business appears in Viadere's neutral style. Related: [Loops](/products/loops/) and [verification badges](/security/verification-badges/).
