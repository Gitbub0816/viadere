::: section
::: prose
A Loop is an optional, business-authored interaction: a short form, a summary and an outcome, such as scheduling a pickup or acknowledging an estimate. The business chooses fields, steps and actions. Viadere owns every component that draws them.

::: callout
**Built, not released.** Hosted Loops, the review pipeline and Loop Studio are built and tested on the server. Loops are not available to any business or customer, the app screens have not been run on a device, and no date is promised.
:::

## Four steps, *and a person reviews every one.*
:::

::: steps four
::: card
### Assemble
In Loop Studio, a business starts from a template and arranges approved fields and actions. There is no free-form schema and no code.
:::
::: card
### Review
An automated policy check is a hard gate. Then a Viadere reviewer, who is never the author, approves or rejects. Every revision is reviewed again.
:::
::: card
### Publish
Viadere signs the approved revision. A first publication starts with an allow-list only, and a rollout percentage is raised from there.
:::
::: card
### Answer
The customer's action goes to the business's inbox. Staff accept, reject or ask for more, and the customer sees that outcome.
:::

## Declarative data, *never code.*
:::

::: grid grid-2 gap-lg
::: card
### {icon:code} Nothing executable
A Loop is declarative data. No business-provided code, HTML, script or arbitrary link runs on a customer's device.
:::
::: card
### {icon:store} Always “Built by”
Every Loop names the business that made it, even one drawn entirely in Viadere's default style. The name comes from the verified business record, not from the Loop.
:::
::: card
### {icon:sliders} A closed set of actions
A button binds to an action Viadere registered: acknowledge, confirm attendance, request a callback, send an inquiry, check in, select an option or a pickup time, review an estimate. A business cannot define its own.
:::
::: card
### {icon:user} Off until the customer turns it on
Loops are off for every customer by default. Turning them on means reading a short explanation, answering comprehension questions and agreeing. Each business is then authorized separately, and any grant can be revoked.
:::
:::
:::

::: section alt
::: grid grid-2 gap-lg
::: card
### {icon:bell} Honest outcomes
A request sent is not a request accepted. A Loop shows pending, accepted, rejected, timed out or unknown as separate states, and a notification about one carries no business name and no content.

Customers can report a Loop. Enough distinct reports suspend it automatically, and Viadere can pause one Loop, one business or all of them.
:::
::: card
### {icon:x} What Loops do not do
- Loops are not for healthcare providers and never collect health information.
- Loops never take a payment. Viadere is never the merchant of record.
- A Loop cannot run an identity or age check. Only Viadere's own Verify can.
- Only the hosted mode is built. Delivery to a business's own endpoint is not built, and neither is any commerce connector.
- Approving an estimate inside a Loop is not offered yet.
:::
:::

Core features do not depend on Loops: messaging, approvals and rewards work with no Loop at all. Related: [Brand Kits](/products/brand-kits/) and the [security overview](/security/).
