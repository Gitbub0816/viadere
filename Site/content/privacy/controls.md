title: Your controls

description: Consent, connections and notifications.
---
- **Connect on purpose.** You connect to a business by handle, code, QR or button. It cannot reach you until you do.
- **Consent per purpose.** Security, account service, transactional, support, marketing and rewards are separate grants. You can revoke marketing without disabling essential security notices.
- **Disconnect.** You can disconnect from a business, and its pending requests are cancelled.
- **Notifications.** Viadere respects system notification settings and Focus, and by default interrupts only when action is required.
- **Devices.** You can see and manage the devices that can approve for you. [PRODUCT: confirm the device management screens at launch.]
- **Text messages.** Reply STOP to stop messages by text, HELP for help.

Requests that need more than a setting, such as access or deletion, are covered under [requests](/privacy/requests/).
