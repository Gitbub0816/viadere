Most choices are in the app. For anything else, see [requests](/privacy/requests/).

## Connections and consent

- **Connect on purpose.** You connect to a business by handle, permanent code, QR code, link or a "Connect with Viadere" button. It cannot reach you until you do.
- **Consent per purpose.** Security, account service, transactional, support, marketing and rewards are separate. You can revoke marketing without disabling essential security notices, and revoking one purpose never changes another.
- **Consent history.** The app shows what you agreed to, when and in which wording version.
- **Disconnect.** Disconnecting from a business cancels its pending requests. It does not delete the business's own records about you. Ask the business about those.
- **Report.** You can deny a request or report it as suspicious. Three denials or reports in an hour pause that business's requests to you.

## Approvals

- **Freeze.** You can freeze approvals for your whole account or for one business at any time, from a registered device. While frozen, results are refused. Lifting a freeze needs a trusted device and a fresh biometric check.
- **Prompt-fatigue limits.** Viadere warns you from the third request in an hour, refuses more than six, and allows at most three pending at once per business.
- **Review before you approve.** Check the business name, the details and the expiry. If it is unfamiliar, deny it and report it.

## Devices and recovery

- **Devices.** See and remove the devices that can approve for you. A new device passes through a cool-down before approving high-risk requests, and your other devices are told so you can cancel.
- **Recovery codes.** Ten single-use recovery codes and a recovery ID are shown once at sign-up. We store only hashes. A recovery registers a new passkey that cannot sign in for 72 hours, while your existing devices are told and can cancel it.
- **App lock.** You can require Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode to open the app.

## Loops

- Loops are optional workflows that a business builds and Viadere reviews before they are published. They are **off** until you turn them on in Settings.
- Even with Loops on, a business's Loops are available only after you allow that business. You can stop allowing it, or turn Loops off, at any time. Messages and approvals keep working either way.
- Every Loop shows "Built by" and the business's name. You can report a Loop from its screen.
- Do not enter passwords, payment cards or health details in a Loop. Viadere never asks for them.

## Notifications

- Viadere respects iOS notification settings and Focus. By default it interrupts you only when action is required.
- Live Activities are used only for ongoing events such as a delivery or a trip, and never for marketing, rewards or security.
- Turning notifications off can mean you miss time-limited requests. They expire.

## Text messages

- Reply **STOP** (or STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, OPTOUT or REVOKE) to stop text messages. Reply **HELP** or **INFO** for help. Reply **START**, **UNSTOP** or **YES** only to resume after you opt out.
- Message and data rates may apply. SMS is not end-to-end encrypted. Do not send sensitive information by text.
- Marketing text messages are sent only if you gave marketing consent that covers text for that business.
- Your opt-out is kept as a suppression record so that it is honored even if you later delete your account.

## Your data

- **Download my data.** Settings, Download my data. The file is encrypted to a key only your iPhone holds, so Viadere cannot read it. It is available for 7 days, up to 3 requests a day.
- **Delete my account.** Settings, Delete account. See [retention](/privacy/retention/) for what that erases.
