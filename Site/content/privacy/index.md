## The short version

- **We carry requests, not a profile of you.** Viadere delivers sign-in approvals, purchase authorizations, messages, orders and rewards between you and verified businesses. We do not sell personal information, do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and do not run advertising.
- **Businesses see a pseudonym.** Each business sees a reference to you that is different for every business, the decisions you make and the messages you send it. It does not see your Viadere identity or your phone number unless you give it.
- **Your keys stay on your device.** Approval signing keys live in your iPhone's Secure Enclave. We keep the public keys.
- **No email, no password.** Consumer accounts use a passkey. A phone number is optional and is only needed to use Viadere by text.
- **Text is not private from carriers.** SMS is not end-to-end encrypted. Sensitive approvals never happen by text.
- **You are in control.** Download your data, delete your account, disconnect from a business, change consent by purpose, freeze approvals and reply STOP, all from the app or your phone.

## Principles

1. **Minimize what we must possess.** If a device, an issuer or a verification provider can hold something, we prefer that to holding it ourselves.
2. **Pseudonymous toward businesses.** A business can recognize you as the same person across its own conversations, and cannot compare notes with another business to link you.
3. **Purpose-bound.** Security, account service, transactional, support, marketing and rewards are separate permissions. Marketing consent never switches on security notices, and revoking it never switches them off.
4. **Encrypted by default where we hold it.** Phone numbers, message bodies and request details are encrypted before they reach the database, with keys held outside it.
5. **Retention by class, not one number.** Each class of data has a stated period and a stated reason, and the schedule is published.
6. **Honest limits.** SMS is not end-to-end encrypted. Deleting data on our servers does not revoke a credential held by someone else. Backups age out on a schedule. We say so.

## What Viadere does not do

| We do not | What that means |
|---|---|
| Sell or share your data for advertising | No advertising identifiers, no ad networks, no tracking domains declared in the app |
| Read your contacts or location | The app does not ask for either. A business may include an approximate location in a request so you can decide |
| Use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs | None in the app or on the website |
| Keep your ID images, selfie or birthdate | If Verify launches, evidence goes from your device to the verification provider, and we keep only a minimal result |
| Hold your private keys | Approval and passkey private keys stay on your device |
| Take payments | Viadere never accepts, processes or holds a payment and is never the seller. A business that uses Shopify sends you to its own Shopify checkout |
| Allow health information or healthcare providers | Healthcare providers may not use Viadere, and no business may use it to collect health information |
| Turn on Loops for you | Optional business workflows are off until you turn them on and allow each business. Every one shows who built it |
| Track you on the website | The site sets no cookies and loads nothing from other companies |
