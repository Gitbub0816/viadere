You can ask to access, correct, delete or take a copy of your data, to withdraw consent or to object, and to appeal a decision. This page says how, and what the law of your region adds. The binding text is in the [Privacy Notice](/legal/privacy-notice/).

## In the app

| You want to | Do this |
|---|---|
| Get a copy of your data | Settings, Download my data. The file is encrypted to your device and available for 7 days |
| Delete your account | Settings, Delete account |
| Change or withdraw consent | Open a business, change its purposes. Revoking marketing never turns off security notices |
| Stop text messages | Reply STOP |
| Remove a device or freeze approvals | Settings, Devices; the freeze control |

## By contacting us

For anything the app does not cover, write to **[PRIVACY-CONTACT-PLACEHOLDER]**. Because consumer accounts have no email address, we may ask you to send the request from the app, from your trusted device or from your linked phone number so we can confirm it is you. We ask only for enough to find your records and verify you. We do not ask for a password, a code or an approval by text or email, and we do not ask for a government ID unless nothing else works.

## Who holds what

- **Viadere** holds your account, devices, consent and security records, and the messages and requests it carries.
- **Each business** holds its own records about you. Ask the business for those.
- **Where Viadere carries data for a business** (as its processor or service provider), we may refer your request to the business.
- **Deleting data on Viadere's servers does not revoke a credential held by a third party,** such as an account at a business, a Wallet pass already on your phone or a passkey registered at a website.

## Regional rights

### European Economic Area, United Kingdom and similar

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection to processing based on legitimate interests, withdrawal of consent, and not being subject to a decision based solely on automated processing with legal or similarly significant effects (we make none). You may complain to your supervisory authority, such as the UK Information Commissioner's Office or the authority in your member state. [COUNSEL: lead authority, representatives and data protection officer.]

### California

The right to know, delete and correct personal information, to opt out of sale and sharing, to limit use of sensitive personal information, and not to be discriminated against. We do not sell or share personal information and do not offer financial incentives. You can use an authorized agent. See the [California notice at collection](/legal/privacy-notice/#23-california-notice-at-collection).

### Other US states

Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Oregon, Utah, Montana and others give rights to access, correct, delete and obtain a copy of personal data, and to opt out of targeted advertising, sale and certain profiling. We do none of those. You may appeal a refusal by writing to [PRIVACY-CONTACT-PLACEHOLDER] with "Appeal" in the subject, and we reply within 60 days. [COUNSEL: state-by-state applicability.]

### Illinois and other biometric laws

If Verify launches and you choose the document method, a verification provider captures biometric data on our behalf. We ask for your written consent first, do not sell it and delete the session after the result is read. See the biometric notice in the Privacy Notice. You can use an Apple Wallet ID where available instead.

### Nevada

We do not sell covered information.

## What we may keep after you ask us to delete

We may keep data we need for a legal duty, to detect fraud or security incidents, or to establish or defend a legal claim. Signed decisions and audit events are kept in minimized form for the periods in [retention](/privacy/retention/). An opt-out from text messages is kept as a suppression record so that it is honored. Deleted data can remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days.

## Appeals and complaints

If we refuse a request, we tell you why. Write to [PRIVACY-CONTACT-PLACEHOLDER] with "Appeal" in the subject. If you are still dissatisfied, you may contact your state attorney general or your supervisory authority.

You may use an authorized agent. Tell us in writing, and we may ask you to confirm the agent's authority and your own identity.
